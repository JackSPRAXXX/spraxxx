Frequently Asked Questions

What is Mail-Safe™?

Mail-Safe™ is cryptographic proof that email came from who it claims. Every message includes a signed receipt you can verify independently — no trust required.

How does human override work?

If you lose your key or need to revoke it, a human review process can restore access. No algorithm-only lockouts. We verify your identity through multiple channels before issuing new credentials.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes! Creator plans include custom domain support with full Mail-Safe™ signing. You keep your brand while getting verified email infrastructure.

What happens to my data?

Your data stays yours. We never sell, broker, or harvest your information. Export everything anytime using open protocols.

Is SPRAXXX end-to-end encrypted?

Mail-Safe™ provides cryptographic sender verification. For E2EE messaging between SPRAXXX users, keys are exchanged automatically. For external recipients, standard TLS applies.

Can I import my existing email?

Yes. Creator plans include migration tools to import from Gmail, Outlook, and other IMAP providers. Historical messages won't have Mail-Safe™ signatures, but new mail will.
